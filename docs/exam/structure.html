選修項目在考試當日才選定，毋須在報名時決定。

考評局反覆強調的答題原則

以下幾點見於 2023 至 2025 年每一份簡報，適用於全卷，單元 A 尤其明顯。

  1. 答案要扣緊題目情境。 評卷員不會猜測考生的意思；答案只複述常識、過於籠統或照抄題目，一律不給分。
  2. 要求兩個答案時，兩個答案必須屬於不同類別。 同一類別的答案只得 1 分。例如「在不同網站核對」和「向老師查證」都屬「多方核對」。
  3. 用專業術語。 修讀 ICT 的考生應以技術角度作答，例如寫「固定值檢查」而不是「檢查是否正確」。
  4. 多分題有層次。 第一分通常容易取得，其後各分要求更深入的理解。
  5. 計算題要列式和寫單位。 缺少單位會失去答案分。
  6. 比較題要用比較級。 例如寫「較高的時鐘頻率」而不是「高時鐘頻率」〔2025 卷一乙 5(a)〕。
  7. 用報考語文作答。 中文卷以中文作答；函數名稱、SQL 指令及常見縮寫（如 SSL、RSI）可照寫英文。

Paper Content Weighting Time
Paper 1A Compulsory Part, multiple-choice questions (40 questions) 22% 2 hours in total
Paper 1B Compulsory Part, short and structured questions (question-answer book) 33%
Paper 2 Elective Part, choose two of three (our school: 2A Databases, 2B Web Application Development) 25% 1 hour 30 minutes
School-based Assessment Two guided tasks 20% —

Electives are chosen in the examination itself, not at registration.

Answering principles the HKEAA keeps repeating

These points appear in every briefing from 2023 to 2025. They apply to the whole paper and are especially visible in Module A.

  1. Fit your answer to the scenario in the question. Markers do not guess what you mean; answers that only repeat common sense, are too general, or copy the question score nothing.
  2. When two answers are required, they must come from different categories. Two answers in the same category score only 1 mark. For example, "check other websites" and "ask a teacher" both fall under "cross-checking".
  3. Use technical terms. ICT candidates are expected to answer from a technical angle, e.g. write "fixed value check" rather than "check whether it is correct".
  4. Multi-mark questions are graded. The first mark is usually easy; later marks need deeper understanding.
  5. Show working and units in calculations. A missing unit loses the answer mark.
  6. Use comparatives in comparison questions. Write "a higher clock rate", not "a high clock rate" [2025 P1B 5(a)].
  7. Answer in the language you entered for. Answers not written in the language medium entered will not be marked — for example, a Chinese axis name in the English paper [2023 P1B 2(e)]. Function names, SQL keywords and common abbreviations (e.g. SSL, RSI) are written as they are.
